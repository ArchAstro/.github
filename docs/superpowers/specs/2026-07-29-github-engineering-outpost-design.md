# GitHub Engineering Outpost Design

## Goal

Make the ArchAstro GitHub organization a credible engineering-first entry
point for the company, its product, and its public developer tools.

## Positioning

ArchAstro is the company. Its broad thesis is helping engineering teams scale
in the age of AI. ArchAgents is a product made by ArchAstro and keeps its
specific positioning around customer-facing engineering work.

The profile should demonstrate the company thesis through shipped public work
instead of announcing internal projects or making broad claims. It links to:

- `archastro.ai` for the company and team
- `archagents.com` for the commercial product
- `oss.archastro.ai` for supported open-source projects
- public documentation, developer tools, and SDKs

## Community defaults

The organization repository provides concise default contribution, conduct,
security, and pull-request guidance. Individual repositories can override these
files when they need project-specific instructions.
